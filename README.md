# The Walking Dead

## A. Introduction

*The Walking Dead* is an American post-apocalyptic horror television
series developed by Frank Darabont and based on the comic book series
created by Robert Kirkman, Tony Moore, and Charlie Adlard. It follows a
group of survivors trying to stay alive in a world overrun by reanimated
corpses known as walkers. The series focuses not only on the danger of
the undead but also on the conflicts, relationships, and difficult
choices people face when society collapses.

-   **Genre:** Horror, post-apocalyptic fiction, survival drama,
    thriller
-   **Original release:** October 31, 2010 -- November 20, 2022
-   **Seasons:** 11
-   **Episodes:** 177

## B. Synopsis

The story begins when sheriff's deputy Rick Grimes wakes up in a
hospital after being in a coma and discovers that the world has been
devastated by a mysterious outbreak. As he searches for his family, he
encounters other survivors and gradually becomes part of a group trying
to find safety. The group travels through different locations, including
camps, farms, prisons, and settlements, while avoiding walkers and other
threats. As the story continues, the survivors face dangerous
communities and leaders whose decisions often put innocent people at
risk. They must deal with loss, betrayal, and disagreements while trying
to protect one another and build a future. Over time, the series
explores how survival can change people and asks whether humanity can
remain good in a world where almost everything has fallen apart.

## C. Characters

1.  **Rick Grimes** -- A former sheriff's deputy who becomes one of the
    main leaders of the survivors. He is determined to protect his
    family and group, even when he has to make difficult decisions.
2.  **Daryl Dixon** -- A skilled hunter and tracker who is initially
    distant and rough around the edges. He gradually becomes a loyal and
    dependable member of the group.
3.  **Michonne** -- A strong and independent survivor who uses a katana
    to fight walkers. Although she is guarded at first, she develops
    close relationships with the people she trusts.
4.  **Carol Peletier** -- A survivor who changes greatly throughout the
    series. She grows from a frightened and quiet person into a
    resourceful, intelligent, and capable fighter.
5.  **Glenn Rhee** -- A kind and courageous survivor known for his quick
    thinking and resourcefulness. He often tries to do what is right and
    cares deeply about the people around him.

## D. Personal Review

I chose *The Walking Dead* because I enjoy stories that combine horror,
survival, and character development. I like that the series is not only
about fighting zombies; it also shows how people react when they lose
the safety and structure of normal life.

Three things I particularly like about the series are:

1.  **Character development:** The characters change as they experience
    difficult situations, and their relationships make the story more
    meaningful.
2.  **Survival and world-building:** The different communities and
    locations show how people attempt to rebuild society in their own
    ways.
3.  **Emotional moments:** The series has memorable scenes involving
    friendship, sacrifice, conflict, and loss.

> "We are the ones who live."

This line reflects one of the series' central ideas: surviving is not
just about staying alive, but also about deciding what kind of person
you want to be.

**Personal rating: 9/10**

I would recommend *The Walking Dead* to people who enjoy horror,
suspense, survival stories, and character-driven dramas. Some parts may
feel slow, especially in certain seasons, and the series contains
graphic violence. However, its characters and emotional storylines make
it worth watching.
